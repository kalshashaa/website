# Aali Aljazerah Initial Design Direction

Status: Local design proposal for owner review. Not approved for public launch.

The approved scope is a bilingual business contracting and maintenance website. Its visual language comes from the existing arch-shaped logo, navy-and-gold company profile, building elevations and material samples.

## Visual system

- Deep navy `#102A42` anchors the header, introduction, and footer; the original logo retains its own navy background.
- Gold `#C6A04B` marks small structural details and primary buttons, with navy button text for contrast.
- White `#FFFFFF` provides the main content canvas.
- Cool mist `#F1F4F6` separates secondary content.
- Slate `#526577` is reserved for readable supporting copy on light backgrounds.
- Pale navy `#B8C8D6` is supporting text on dark surfaces.

Typography: IBM Plex Sans Arabic for Arabic, Manrope for English. Both are self-hosted from installed font packages. Headings use deliberate medium/bold weights; body copy remains comfortably spaced. Arabic text is right aligned and English left aligned.

## Composition

The opening uses an asymmetrical architectural frame: text on navy beside an illustrative commercial building image, with a single gold corner detail. This is the signature, rather than a collection of repeated decorative effects. White service sections use a compact catalogue with equal treatment of every service. Authentic project photos have separate labels and factual work descriptions.

```text
Header: original logo | four page links | language switch
Home: introduction + architectural image
      service catalogue
      company approach + regional coverage
      three authentic project summaries
      contact-page link
Footer: identity + page navigation; no contact number
```

The UI UX Pro Max search returned a relevant low-complexity corporate layout recommendation but generic gray/orange colors and Latin fonts. Those colors/fonts were rejected in favor of the owner's approved palette and Arabic typography. No stats, client-logo strip, CEO section, emergency promise, or floating contact control is included.

Only subtle interactive transitions are planned. No automatically playing video, parallax, autoplay carousel, or mandatory entrance animations. Reduced motion is respected.

The first eight-page implementation is a local visual/content review build. Connecting and validating the real CMS account is the next phase after this review; the design build must not imply that cloud editing already works.
